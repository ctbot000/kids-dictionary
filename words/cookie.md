# 🍪 cookie

/ˈkʊki/ · **쿠**키

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**쿠키**

## English meaning

A cookie is a small, flat, sweet snack.
People bake it in an oven.
Many kids eat cookies with milk.

## Examples

- Can I have a **cookie**?
  - **쿠키** 하나 먹어도 돼요?
- I eat a **cookie** with milk.
  - 나는 우유와 함께 **쿠키**를 먹어요.
