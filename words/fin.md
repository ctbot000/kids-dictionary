# fin

/fɪn/ · **핀**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**지느러미**

## English meaning

A fin is a flat part on a fish's body.
A fish uses its fins to swim.
A shark has a fin on its back.

## Examples

- The fish moves its **fin**.
  - 물고기가 **지느러미**를 움직여요.
- I see a shark **fin**!
  - 상어 **지느러미**가 보여요!
