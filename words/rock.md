# 🪨 rock

/rɑːk/ · **락**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**바위**

## English meaning

A rock is a hard, solid piece of stone.
Rocks can be big or small.
You can find rocks on the ground and in the mountains.

## Examples

- I sit on a big **rock**.
  - 나는 큰 **바위**에 앉아요.
- I found a red **rock** by the river.
  - 나는 강가에서 빨간 **돌**을 찾았어요.
