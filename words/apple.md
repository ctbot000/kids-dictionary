# 🍎 apple

/ˈæpəl/ · **애**플

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**사과**

## English meaning

An apple is a round fruit. It grows on a tree.
It can be red, green, or yellow. It tastes sweet.

## Examples

- I eat an **apple** every day.
  - 나는 매일 **사과**를 하나씩 먹어요.
- This **apple** is red and sweet.
  - 이 **사과**는 빨갛고 달아요.
