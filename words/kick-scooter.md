# 🛴 kick scooter

/kɪk ˈskuːtər/ · **킥** 스쿠터

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**킥보드**

## English meaning

A kick scooter is a toy with two wheels and a handle.
You stand on it and push the ground with one foot.
Kids ride kick scooters at the park.

## Examples

- I ride my **kick scooter** to school.
  - 나는 **킥보드**를 타고 학교에 가요.
- Look at her red **kick scooter**!
  - 그녀의 빨간 **킥보드** 좀 봐요!
