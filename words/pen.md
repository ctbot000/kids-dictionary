# 🖊️ pen

/pɛn/ · **펜**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**펜**

## English meaning

A pen is a tool for writing.
It has ink inside.
You hold it and write words.

## Examples

- I write with a blue **pen**.
  - 나는 파란 **펜**으로 글씨를 써요.
- Can I use your **pen**?
  - 네 **펜** 좀 써도 돼요?
