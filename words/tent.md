# ⛺ tent

/tɛnt/ · **텐**트

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**텐트**

## English meaning

A tent is a small house made of cloth.
People sleep in a tent when they stay outside.
You can carry it in a bag.

## Examples

- We sleep in a **tent**.
  - 우리는 **텐트**에서 자요.
- Our **tent** is blue.
  - 우리 **텐트**는 파란색이에요.
