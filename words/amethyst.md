# amethyst

/ˈæmɪθɪst/ · **애**머씨스트

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**자수정**

## English meaning

An amethyst is a purple jewel.
It is a hard, shiny stone.
People wear amethysts in rings and necklaces.

## Examples

- Her ring has a purple **amethyst**.
  - 그녀의 반지에는 보라색 **자수정**이 있어요.
- An **amethyst** is shiny and purple.
  - **자수정**은 반짝이고 보라색이에요.
