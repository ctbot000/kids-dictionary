# 🌌 universe

/ˈjuːnɪvɜːrs/ · **유**니버스

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**우주**

## English meaning

The universe is all of space and everything in it.
It has all the stars and planets. The sun and the Earth are in it, too.
It is very, very big.

## Examples

- There are many stars in the **universe**.
  - **우주**에는 별이 아주 많아요.
- I read a book about the **universe**.
  - 나는 **우주**에 관한 책을 읽었어요.
