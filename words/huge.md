# huge

/hjuːdʒ/ · **휴**지

*adjective* · 형용사

## 한국어 뜻

**아주 큰, 거대한**

## English meaning

If something is huge, it is very, very big.
An elephant is huge. A mouse is not huge. It is small.

## Examples

- Look at that **huge** whale!
  - 저 **거대한** 고래 좀 봐요!
- Our school has a **huge** playground.
  - 우리 학교에는 **아주 큰** 운동장이 있어요.

   - very, very big
