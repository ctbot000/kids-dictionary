# 🥛 milk

/mɪlk/ · **밀크**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**우유**

## English meaning

Milk is a white drink.
Many kids drink it every day.
Most milk comes from cows.

## Examples

- I drink **milk** every day.
  - 나는 매일 **우유**를 마셔요.
- I put **milk** on my cereal.
  - 나는 시리얼에 **우유**를 넣어요.
