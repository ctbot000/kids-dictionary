# 🕹️ video arcade

/ˈvɪdioʊ ɑːrˈkeɪd/ · **비**디오 아**케**이드

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**오락실**

## English meaning

A video arcade is a place with many video games.
People play the games on big screens and machines.
Kids often go there with their friends.

## Examples

- We go to the **video arcade** on Saturday.
  - 우리는 토요일에 **오락실**에 가요.
- The **video arcade** has many fun games.
  - **오락실**에는 재미있는 게임이 많아요.
