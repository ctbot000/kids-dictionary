# 🦟 mosquito

/məˈskiːtoʊ/ · 머**스**키토

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**모기**

## English meaning

A mosquito is a tiny bug that flies.
It bites people and animals to drink their blood.
Its bites can be itchy.

## Examples

- A **mosquito** is flying near my ear.
  - **모기**가 내 귀 옆에서 날아다녀요.
- The **mosquito** bit my arm.
  - **모기**가 내 팔을 물었어요.
