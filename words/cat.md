# 🐱 cat

/kæt/ · **캣**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**고양이**

## English meaning

A cat is a small animal with soft hair.
Many people have a cat as a pet. A cat says "meow."

## Examples

- I have a **cat**.
  - 나는 **고양이**를 키워요.
- The **cat** is sleeping on my bed.
  - **고양이**가 내 침대에서 자고 있어요.
