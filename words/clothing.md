# 👕 clothing

/ˈkloʊðɪŋ/ · **클**로우딩

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**옷**

## English meaning

Clothing is things you wear on your body.
Shirts, pants, and hats are clothing.
You wear clothing to keep warm and to look nice.

## Examples

- I put on warm **clothing** in winter.
  - 나는 겨울에 따뜻한 **옷**을 입어요.
- This store sells red **clothing**.
  - 이 가게는 빨간 **옷**을 팔아요.
