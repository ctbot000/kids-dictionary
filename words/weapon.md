# weapon

/ˈwɛpən/ · **웨**펀

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**무기**

## English meaning

A weapon is a tool people use to fight.
A sword is a weapon.
Weapons can hurt people.
Kids should never play with real weapons.

## Examples

- The knight holds a **weapon**.
  - 기사는 **무기**를 들고 있어요.
- A sword is a **weapon**.
  - 칼은 **무기**예요.
