# 🧪 potion

/ˈpoʊʃən/ · **포**우션

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**마법 물약**

## English meaning

A potion is a drink made with magic.
It can heal you or make you strong.
In stories, a witch brews a potion in a pot.

## Examples

- The witch made a green **potion**.
  - 마녀가 초록색 **마법 물약**을 만들었어요.
- The knight drank a **potion** and felt strong.
  - 기사는 **마법 물약**을 마시고 힘이 세졌어요.
