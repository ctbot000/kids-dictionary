# pet

/pɛt/ · **펫**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**반려동물**

## English meaning

A pet is an animal that lives with you at home.
You give it food and play with it.
Dogs and cats are pets.

## Examples

- My **pet** is a small dog.
  - 내 **반려동물**은 작은 강아지예요.
- I feed my **pet** every morning.
  - 나는 매일 아침 **반려동물**에게 밥을 줘요.
