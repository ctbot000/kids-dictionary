# wrap

/ræp/ · **랩**

*verb* · 동사

## 한국어 뜻

**포장하다**

## English meaning

When you wrap something, you cover it all around.
You can wrap a present in paper.
You can wrap yourself in a blanket.

## Examples

- I **wrap** the present in blue paper.
  - 나는 선물을 파란 종이로 **포장해요**.
- Mom will **wrap** my sandwich.
  - 엄마가 내 샌드위치를 **포장해 줘요**.
