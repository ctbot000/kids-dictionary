# piglet

/ˈpɪɡlət/ · **피**글릿

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**아기 돼지**

## English meaning

A piglet is a baby pig.
It is small and pink.
It says oink.
A piglet grows up to be a pig.

## Examples

- The **piglet** is playing in the mud.
  - **아기 돼지**가 진흙에서 놀아요.
- The **piglet** is sleeping next to its mom.
  - **아기 돼지**가 엄마 옆에서 자요.
