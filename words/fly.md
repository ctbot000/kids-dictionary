# 🪰 fly

/flaɪ/ · **플라이**

*verb, noun* · 동사, 명사

## 한국어 뜻

1. **날다**
2. **파리**

## English meaning

1. When a bird flies, it moves through the air. Birds use their wings to fly. Planes fly in the sky too.
2. A fly is a small bug. It has two thin wings. It buzzes around food.

## Examples

1. Birds **fly** in the sky.
   - 새들이 하늘을 **날아요**.
2. There is a **fly** on my cake!
   - 내 케이크에 **파리**가 앉아 있어요!

## Quiz

   - through the water
   - under the ground
   - through the air
2. What do birds use to fly?
   - their wings
   - their teeth
   - their spoons
3. What is a fly?
   - a big fish
   - a small bug
   - a tall tree
