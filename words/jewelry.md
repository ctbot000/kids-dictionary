# 💍 jewelry

/ˈdʒuːəlri/ · **주**얼리

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**보석**

## English meaning

Jewelry is pretty things that you wear.
Rings and necklaces are jewelry.
Jewelry is often made of gold or silver.

## Examples

- This necklace is **jewelry**.
  - 이 목걸이는 **보석**이에요.
- I keep my **jewelry** in a box.
  - 나는 **보석**을 상자에 넣어 둬요.
