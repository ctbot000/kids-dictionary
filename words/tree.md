# 🌳 tree

/triː/ · **트리**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**나무**

## English meaning

A tree is a very tall plant.
It has brown branches and green leaves.
Birds can make a home in a tree.

## Examples

- A bird is in the **tree**.
  - 새가 **나무**에 있어요.
- We sit under the **tree**.
  - 우리는 **나무** 아래에 앉아요.
