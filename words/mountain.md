# ⛰️ mountain

/ˈmaʊntən/ · **마운**튼

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**산**

## English meaning

A mountain is a very, very high hill.
It is made of rock and earth.
Some mountains have snow on top.

## Examples

- We climb the **mountain**.
  - 우리는 **산**에 올라가요.
- There is snow on the **mountain**.
  - **산** 위에 눈이 있어요.
