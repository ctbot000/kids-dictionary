# 🧒 kid

/kɪd/ · **킷**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**아이**

## English meaning

A kid is a young person.
A kid is not a grown-up yet.
Kids play, learn, and go to school.

## Examples

- That **kid** has a red hat.
  - 저 **아이**는 빨간 모자를 썼어요.
- Every **kid** likes to play.
  - 모든 **아이**는 노는 걸 좋아해요.

## Quiz

1. What is a kid?
   - an old tree
   - a young person
   - a big car
2. Is a kid a grown-up?
   - Yes, always.
   - Yes, like a dad.
   - No, not yet.
3. What do kids do?
   - They drive a bus.
   - They play, learn, and go to school.
   - They live in the sea.
