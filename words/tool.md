# 🔧 tool

/tuːl/ · **툴**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**도구**

## English meaning

A tool is a thing you hold to help you do a job.
A hammer and scissors are tools.
You use a tool with your hands.

## Examples

- Dad uses a **tool** to fix the chair.
  - 아빠는 의자를 고칠 때 **도구**를 써요.
- Scissors are a useful **tool**.
  - 가위는 쓸모 있는 **도구**예요.
