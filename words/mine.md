# mine

/maɪn/ · **마인**

*pronoun, noun* · 대명사, 명사

## 한국어 뜻

1. **내 것**
2. **광산**

## English meaning

1. If something is mine, it belongs to me.
   It is a thing that I have, like my toy or my cup.
   When you talk about your own things, you say mine.
2. A mine is a deep hole under the ground.
   People dig in a mine to find gold.
   It is dark inside.

## Examples

1. This red cup is **mine**.
   - 이 빨간 컵은 **내 것**이에요.
2. It is dark inside the **mine**.
   - **광산** 안은 어두워요.

## Quiz

1. If a cup is mine, whose cup is it?
   - It is the moon's cup.
   - It is my cup.
   - It is a fish's cup.
2. What do people dig for in a mine?
   - gold
   - ice cream
   - shoes
3. Where is a mine?
   - up in the sky
   - in a tree
   - under the ground
