# mine

/maɪn/ · **마인**

*pronoun* · 대명사

## 한국어 뜻

**내 것**

## English meaning

If something is mine, it belongs to me.
It is a thing that I have, like my toy or my cup.
When you talk about your own things, you say mine.

## Examples

- This red cup is **mine**.
  - 이 빨간 컵은 **내 것**이에요.
- Look! The blue bike is **mine**.
  - 봐요! 파란 자전거는 **내 것**이에요.

## Quiz

1. What does mine mean?
   - It belongs to me.
   - It is very big.
   - It is very cold.
2. If a toy is mine, whose toy is it?
   - It is the moon's toy.
   - It is my toy.
   - It is a fish's toy.
3. Which sentence says the ball belongs to me?
   - The ball can sing.
   - The ball is a fish.
   - The ball is mine.

<details>
<summary>Answers · 정답</summary>

1. It belongs to me.
2. It is my toy.
3. The ball is mine.
