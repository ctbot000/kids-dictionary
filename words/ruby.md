# ruby

/ˈruːbi/ · **루**비

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**루비**

## English meaning

A ruby is a red jewel.
It is a hard, shiny stone.
People wear rubies in rings and necklaces.

## Examples

- Her ring has a red **ruby**.
  - 그녀의 반지에는 빨간 **루비**가 있어요.
- A **ruby** is very red and shiny.
  - **루비**는 아주 빨갛고 반짝여요.
