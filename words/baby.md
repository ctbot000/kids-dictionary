# 👶 baby

/ˈbeɪbi/ · **베**이비

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**아기**

## English meaning

A baby is a very young person.
A baby is smaller than a kid.
A baby cannot walk or talk yet.
Babies drink milk and sleep a lot.

## Examples

- The **baby** is sleeping.
  - **아기**가 자고 있어요.
- My little sister is a **baby**.
  - 내 여동생은 **아기**예요.
