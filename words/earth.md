# 🌍 earth

/ɜːrθ/ · **얼쓰**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**지구**

## English meaning

The Earth is the planet where we live.
It is round and has land and water.
People, animals, and plants live on Earth.

## Examples

- We live on **Earth**.
  - 우리는 **지구**에 살아요.
- The **Earth** goes around the sun.
  - **지구**는 태양 둘레를 돌아요.
