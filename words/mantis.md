# mantis

/ˈmæntɪs/ · **맨**티스

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**사마귀**

## English meaning

A mantis is a green bug with long front legs.
It sits very still and waits for food.
Then it grabs a small bug very fast.

## Examples

- A **mantis** sits on the green leaf.
  - **사마귀**가 초록 잎에 앉아 있어요.
- Look at the **mantis** holding its legs up!
  - 앞다리를 든 **사마귀** 좀 봐요!
