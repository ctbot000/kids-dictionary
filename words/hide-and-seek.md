# hide-and-seek

/ˌhaɪd ən ˈsiːk/ · 하이드 앤 **씨**크

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**숨바꼭질**

## English meaning

Hide-and-seek is a game where one player looks for the others.
The other players hide, and the player who looks is the seeker.
Kids play it with friends.

## Examples

- Let's play hide-and-seek in the yard!
  - 마당에서 **숨바꼭질** 하자!
- I found my friend in hide-and-seek.
  - 나는 **숨바꼭질**에서 친구를 찾았어요.
