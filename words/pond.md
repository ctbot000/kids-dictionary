# pond

/pɑːnd/ · **폰**드

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**연못**

## English meaning

A pond is a small lake of water.
It is smaller than a lake.
Frogs and ducks can live in a pond.

## Examples

- Ducks swim in the **pond**.
  - 오리들이 **연못**에서 헤엄쳐요.
- We feed the fish in the **pond**.
  - 우리는 **연못**의 물고기에게 먹이를 줘요.
