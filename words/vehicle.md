# 🚗 vehicle

/ˈviːɪkəl/ · **비**이클

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**탈것**

## English meaning

A vehicle is a thing that carries people or things from place to place.
Cars, buses, and bikes are vehicles.
Most vehicles have wheels.

## Examples

- A bus is a big **vehicle**.
  - 버스는 큰 **탈것**이에요.
- My dad drives a red **vehicle**.
  - 우리 아빠는 빨간 **차**를 운전해요.
