# room

/ruːm/ · **룸**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**방**

## English meaning

A room is one part inside a house.
It has walls, a floor, and a door.
Your bed is in your room.

## Examples

- My **room** has a big window.
  - 내 **방**에는 큰 창문이 있어요.
- This is my **room**.
  - 여기가 내 **방**이에요.
