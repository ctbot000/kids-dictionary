# ⏰ clock

/klɑːk/ · **클**락

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**시계**

## English meaning

A clock is a thing that shows the time.
It has numbers and two hands that move around.
You can hang a clock on a wall.

## Examples

- The **clock** on the wall says three o'clock.
  - 벽에 있는 **시계**가 세 시를 가리켜요.
- I look at the **clock** before school.
  - 나는 학교 가기 전에 **시계**를 봐요.
