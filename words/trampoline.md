# trampoline

/ˈtræmpəliːn/ · 트**램**펄린

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**트램펄린**

## English meaning

A trampoline is a big, round mat.
You jump on it, and it pushes you up.
It helps you jump very high.

## Examples

- I like to jump on the **trampoline**.
  - 나는 **트램펄린** 위에서 뛰는 걸 좋아해요.
- We have a **trampoline** in our yard.
  - 우리 마당에는 **트램펄린**이 있어요.
