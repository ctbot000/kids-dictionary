# ⚔️ sword

/sɔːrd/ · **소**드

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**칼**

## English meaning

A sword is a long, sharp blade with a handle.
Knights used swords long ago.
A sword is a kind of weapon.

## Examples

- The knight has a shiny **sword**.
  - 기사는 반짝이는 **칼**을 가지고 있어요.
- A **sword** is long and sharp.
  - **칼**은 길고 날카로워요.
