# grown-up

/ˈɡroʊnʌp/ · 그**로우**넙

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**어른**

## English meaning

A grown-up is a person who is not a kid anymore.
Your mom and dad are grown-ups.
Grown-ups work and take care of kids.

## Examples

- A **grown-up** is cooking dinner.
  - **어른**이 저녁을 만들어요.
- Ask a **grown-up** for help.
  - **어른**에게 도와 달라고 말해요.
