# duckling

/ˈdʌklɪŋ/ · **덕**링

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**아기 오리**

## English meaning

A duckling is a baby duck.
It is small, soft, and often yellow.
It can swim.
A duckling grows up to be a duck.

## Examples

- The **duckling** is swimming with its mom.
  - **아기 오리**가 엄마와 함께 헤엄쳐요.
- Look at the little yellow **duckling**!
  - 작고 노란 **아기 오리** 좀 봐요!
