# ✏️ pencil

/ˈpensəl/ · **펜**슬

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**연필**

## English meaning

A pencil is a long, thin thing for writing.
You can draw with it, too. Some pencils have an eraser on top.

## Examples

- I write my name with a **pencil**.
  - 나는 **연필**로 내 이름을 써요.
- My **pencil** is broken.
  - 내 **연필**이 부러졌어요.
