# adjective

/ˈædʒɪktɪv/ · **애**직티브

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**꾸미는 말**

## English meaning

An adjective is a word that tells you more about a thing.
Big, red, and happy are adjectives.
In "a red ball," the word red is an adjective.

## Examples

- This is a **big** dog.
  - 이것은 **큰** 개예요.
- I have a **red** ball.
  - 나는 **빨간** 공이 있어요.
