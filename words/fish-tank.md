# 🐠 fish tank

/ˈfɪʃ tæŋk/ · **피쉬** 탱크

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**어항**

## English meaning

A fish tank is a glass box full of water.
Fish swim in it.
People keep one at home to watch the fish.

## Examples

- My **fish tank** is on the table.
  - 내 **어항**은 탁자 위에 있어요.
- There are three fish in the **fish tank**.
  - **어항**에 물고기가 세 마리 있어요.
