# 🍍 pineapple

/ˈpaɪnæpəl/ · **파**인애플

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**파인애플**

## English meaning

A pineapple is a big fruit. Its skin is hard.
It has green leaves on top. It is yellow and sweet inside.

## Examples

- I drink **pineapple** juice.
  - 나는 **파인애플** 주스를 마셔요.
- This **pineapple** is very big.
  - 이 **파인애플**은 정말 커요.
