# invite

/ɪnˈvaɪt/ · 인**바**이트

*verb* · 동사

## 한국어 뜻

**초대하다**

## English meaning

When you invite a friend, you ask them to come.
You can invite friends to your party or your home.
It is a nice way to say, "Please come!"

## Examples

- I want to **invite** you to my birthday party.
  - 내 생일 파티에 너를 **초대하고** 싶어요.
- Mina **invited** me to her house.
  - 미나가 나를 자기 집에 **초대했어요**.
