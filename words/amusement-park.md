# 🎢 amusement park

/əˈmjuːzmənt pɑːrk/ · 어**뮤**즈먼트 파크

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**놀이공원**

## English meaning

An amusement park is a place with many rides and games.
You can ride a roller coaster and eat snacks there.
Families go to an amusement park to have fun together.

## Examples

- We go to the **amusement park** on Sunday.
  - 우리는 일요일에 **놀이공원**에 가요.
- The **amusement park** has a big roller coaster.
  - **놀이공원**에는 큰 롤러코스터가 있어요.
