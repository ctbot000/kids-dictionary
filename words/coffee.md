# ☕ coffee

/ˈkɔːfi/ · **코**피

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**커피**

## English meaning

Coffee is a dark brown drink. It is made from coffee beans.
Many grown-ups drink it in the morning. Most kids do not drink it.

## Examples

- My dad drinks **coffee** every morning.
  - 아빠는 매일 아침 **커피**를 마셔요.
- This **coffee** smells good.
  - 이 **커피**는 냄새가 좋아요.
