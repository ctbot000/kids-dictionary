# flat

/flæt/ · **플랫**

*adjective* · 형용사

## 한국어 뜻

**평평한**

## English meaning

If something is flat, it has no bumps or hills.
A table is flat.
A mountain is not flat.

## Examples

- A pancake is **flat**.
  - 팬케이크는 **평평해요**.
- We play on a **flat** field.
  - 우리는 **평평한** 풀밭에서 놀아요.
