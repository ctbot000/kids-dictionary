# yard

/jɑːrd/ · **야드**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**마당**

## English meaning

A yard is the ground next to a house.
It often has grass and flowers.
Kids can play in a yard.

## Examples

- We play ball in the **yard**.
  - 우리는 **마당**에서 공놀이를 해요.
- There are flowers in our **yard**.
  - 우리 **마당**에는 꽃이 있어요.
