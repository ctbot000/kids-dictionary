# 🕷️ spider

/ˈspaɪdər/ · **스**파이더

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**거미**

## English meaning

A spider is a small animal with eight legs.
Spiders spin webs to catch bugs.
A spider is not an insect.

## Examples

- A **spider** is on the wall.
  - **거미**가 벽에 있어요.
- The **spider** made a web in the corner.
  - **거미**가 구석에 거미줄을 만들었어요.
