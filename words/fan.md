# fan

/fæn/ · **팬**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**선풍기**

## English meaning

A fan is a machine that moves air to make you cool.
It has blades that spin around.
You can turn on a fan on a hot day.

## Examples

- It is hot, so I turn on the **fan**.
  - 더워서 나는 **선풍기**를 켜요.
- The **fan** makes the room feel cool.
  - **선풍기** 덕분에 방이 시원해요.
