# metal

/ˈmɛtəl/ · **메**털

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**금속**

## English meaning

Metal is a hard material that is often shiny.
Gold, silver, and iron are metals.
Spoons and cans are often made of metal.

## Examples

- This spoon is made of **metal**.
  - 이 숟가락은 **금속**으로 만들었어요.
- Gold is a shiny **metal**.
  - 금은 반짝이는 **금속**이에요.
