# often

/ˈɔːfən/ · **오**펀

*adverb* · 부사

## 한국어 뜻

**자주**

## English meaning

If you do something often, you do it many times.
You do not do it just one time.
Kids often play after school.

## Examples

- I **often** eat cookies.
  - 나는 **자주** 쿠키를 먹어요.
- My dog **often** sleeps on my bed.
  - 우리 개는 **자주** 내 침대에서 자요.

## Quiz

1. What does often mean?
   - never
   - many times
   - one time
2. If you often play ball, what is true?
   - You never play ball.
   - You played ball only one time.
   - You play ball many times.
3. Does often mean one time?
   - No, it means many times.
   - Yes, only one time.
   - Yes, it means never.
