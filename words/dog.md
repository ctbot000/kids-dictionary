# 🐶 dog

/dɔːɡ/ · **도**그

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**개**

## English meaning

A dog is an animal with four legs.
Many people have a dog as a pet. A dog says "woof."
It likes to run and play.

## Examples

- I walk my **dog** every day.
  - 나는 매일 **개**를 산책시켜요.
- My **dog** likes to play with a ball.
  - 내 **개**는 공놀이를 좋아해요.
