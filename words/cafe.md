# ☕ cafe

/kæˈfeɪ/ · 캐**페**이

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**카페**

## English meaning

A cafe is a small place to eat and drink.
People go there for coffee, juice, or cake.
You can sit and talk with friends there.

## Examples

- Let's meet at the **cafe**.
  - **카페**에서 만나요.
- My mom and I went to a **cafe**.
  - 엄마랑 나는 **카페**에 갔어요.
