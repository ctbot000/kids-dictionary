# 💎 diamond

/ˈdaɪəmənd/ · **다**이어먼드

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**다이아몬드**

## English meaning

A diamond is a clear, shiny jewel.
It is the hardest stone that people know.
A diamond sparkles when light shines on it.

## Examples

- Her ring has a big **diamond**.
  - 그녀의 반지에는 큰 **다이아몬드**가 있어요.
- A **diamond** sparkles in the sun.
  - **다이아몬드**는 햇빛에 반짝여요.
