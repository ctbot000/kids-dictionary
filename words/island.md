# 🏝️ island

/ˈaɪlənd/ · **아**일런드

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**섬**

## English meaning

An island is land with water all around it.
You can go there by boat. Some islands are big, and some are small.

## Examples

- Jeju is a big **island**.
  - 제주도는 큰 **섬**이에요.
- I want to go to an **island**.
  - 나는 **섬**에 가고 싶어요.

## Quiz

1. What is all around an island?
   - fire
   - water
   - candy
2. What can take you to an island?
   - a boat
   - a spoon
   - a pencil
3. How big is an island?
   - It is always big.
   - It is always small.
   - It can be big or small.
