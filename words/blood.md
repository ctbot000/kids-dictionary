# 🩸 blood

/blʌd/ · 블**러**드

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**피**

## English meaning

Blood is red.
It is inside your body.
It moves around your body all day.
You may see blood when you get a cut.

## Examples

- There is a little **blood** on my finger.
  - 내 손가락에 **피**가 조금 났어요.
- **Blood** is red.
  - **피**는 빨간색이에요.
