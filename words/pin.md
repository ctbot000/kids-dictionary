# 📌 pin

/pɪn/ · **핀**

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**핀**

## English meaning

A pin is a small, thin piece of metal with a sharp point.
People use pins to hold paper or cloth.
Be careful. A pin can poke you.

## Examples

- Mom holds the cloth with a **pin**.
  - 엄마가 **핀**으로 천을 붙여요.
- Be careful with that **pin**!
  - 그 **핀** 조심해요!
