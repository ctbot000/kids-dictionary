# 🍌 banana

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**바나나**

## English meaning

A banana is a long fruit. It has yellow skin.
You take off the skin to eat it. It is soft and sweet inside.

## Examples

- I eat a **banana** for breakfast.
  - 나는 아침으로 **바나나**를 먹어요.
- Monkeys love **bananas**.
  - 원숭이는 **바나나**를 정말 좋아해요.
