# 🏰 castle

/ˈkæsəl/ · **캐**슬

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**성**

## English meaning

A castle is a very big, strong house.
It has tall walls and towers.
Kings and queens lived in castles long ago.

## Examples

- The king lives in a **castle**.
  - 왕은 **성**에 살아요.
- The **castle** has tall towers.
  - **성**에는 높은 탑이 있어요.
