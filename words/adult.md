# adult

/əˈdʌlt/ · 어**덜**트

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**어른**

## English meaning

An adult is a person who is all grown up.
Your mom and dad are adults.
Adults work, drive cars, and take care of kids.

## Examples

- My mom is an **adult**.
  - 우리 엄마는 **어른**이에요.
- Ask an **adult** for help.
  - **어른**에게 도와 달라고 말해요.
