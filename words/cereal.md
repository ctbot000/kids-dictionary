# 🥣 cereal

/ˈsɪriəl/ · **시**리얼

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**시리얼**

## English meaning

Cereal is a food in small, crunchy pieces.
You put it in a bowl and add milk.
Many kids eat cereal at breakfast.

## Examples

- I eat **cereal** every morning.
  - 나는 매일 아침 **시리얼**을 먹어요.
- Can I have more **cereal**?
  - **시리얼** 더 먹어도 돼요?
