# itchy

/ˈɪtʃi/ · **이**치

*adjective* · 형용사

## 한국어 뜻

**가려운**

## English meaning

If your skin is itchy, you want to scratch it.
A mosquito bite can make your skin itchy.
Some sweaters feel itchy, too.

## Examples

- My mosquito bite is so **itchy**!
  - 모기 물린 데가 너무 **가려워요**!
- This sweater is **itchy**.
  - 이 스웨터는 입으면 **가려워요**.
