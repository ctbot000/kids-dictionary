# emerald

/ˈɛmərəld/ · **에**머럴드

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**에메랄드**

## English meaning

An emerald is a green jewel.
It is a hard, shiny stone.
People wear emeralds in rings and necklaces.

## Examples

- The ring has a green **emerald**.
  - 그 반지에는 초록색 **에메랄드**가 있어요.
- Her eyes are as green as an **emerald**.
  - 그녀의 눈은 **에메랄드**처럼 초록색이에요.
