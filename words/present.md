# 🎁 present

/ˈprɛzənt/ · 프**레**즌트

*noun* · 명사

## 한국어 뜻

**선물**

## English meaning

A present is something nice you give to someone.
People give presents on birthdays.
A present is often wrapped in pretty paper.

## Examples

- This is a **present** for you!
  - 이건 너를 위한 **선물**이에요!
- I got a **present** on my birthday.
  - 나는 생일에 **선물**을 받았어요.
